E-Commerce Sales Performance Dashboard

Raw transactional e-commerce data sat in SQL Server with no structured way to surface revenue trends, top products, or country-level performance.

PROJECT 03
Customer Segmentation Dashboard (RFM)
Problem

An e-commerce business lacked visibility into who their most valuable customers were, which customers were at risk of churning, and how purchase behaviour varied across segments.

Solution

Applied the RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) model using SQL to score and classify customers into segments — Champions, Loyal, Potential Loyalists, At Risk, Hibernating. Built a Power BI dashboard with scatter plots, donut charts, and KPI cards. Also produced a formal BRD (Business Requirements Document).

Key Insights

Champions drive the majority of revenue; Potential Loyalists offer strong growth opportunities; higher purchase frequency is the strongest predictor of monetary value.

PROJECT 04
Customer Churn Prediction
Problem

Telco companies lose significant revenue to customer churn, and retaining an existing customer is far cheaper than acquiring a new one. Needed a way to flag at-risk customers before they leave.

Solution

Built and compared three classification models (Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest) on the IBM Telco dataset. Logistic Regression outperformed the ensemble models, revealing that churn behaviour here is largely linear and driven by billing and tenure. Used Random Forest feature importances to interpret drivers, then scored customers by churn probability to build a targeted risk list.

Key Insights

Logistic Regression achieved 79.99% accuracy and 0.84 AUC-ROC, beating Random Forest — proof that simpler models can win when relationships are linear. A 70% risk threshold flagged high-risk customers at 72.83% precision, nearly 3x the baseline churn rate, making retention campaigns far more efficient than blanket outreach.
